Bloom Planner

A pace planner for Pikmin Bloom. Enter your level and your goal date, and every day you'll see whether you're on pace and exactly what it takes.

Download for Android Open on iPhone
Free. No ads, no account, no tracking.

A Target for Every Day

Steps and flowers for today, worked out from your goal date and where you are now. Log each evening and the plan keeps up.

Walking, Biking, or Both

Riding plants flowers fast but earns no steps; the plan knows. Only walk? Turn biking off and every day counts.

Honest About the Weather

Ten years of local weather history show which months you can really ride, so January doesn't get promised the same as June.

In Ten Languages

The same languages as Pikmin Bloom: English, Español, Français, Português, Deutsch, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어, 繁體中文 and 简体中文.

Private by Design

Your log stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is a place's coordinates, sent to Open-Meteo for weather history.

Updates Itself

When a new version or corrected level data is out, the app says so on its home screen. Tap, download, install over the top; your log is kept.

Every Plan Gets a Flower

Each goal is graded by the time it asks of you on a busy day, walking and riding together. The flower shows on the home screen, so how hard the stretch ahead is never comes as a surprise.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone. It's a single APK file, signed, from this project's GitHub releases.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Bloom Planner and set up your level and goal. New versions show up on the home screen when they're out.

On iPhone

  1. Open Bloom Planner on the web in Safari. It's the same app, running in the browser: nothing to download.
  2. Tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the home screen. It runs full screen with its own icon, and your log stays on your iPhone.

The web version has everything except evening reminders, which only the Android app can send. It also works on a computer, in any modern browser.
